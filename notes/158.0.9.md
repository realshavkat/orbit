## New

- Orbit Blocker, rebuilt: it now runs on uBlock Origin with Brave Shields' filter lists and Orbit's own Stealth list. Blocked ads answer as if they had loaded, so sites that check for an ad blocker don't see it.
- Orbit Blocker settings page: protection options, every filter list with an on/off switch, paused sites and your own rules. Open it from the blocker's popup.
- Filter lists update on their own, and new lists arrive without an Orbit update.
- A new Orbit Blocker icon, made from the Orbit logo.
- Microphone, camera and screen: a button left of the address shows what the site can use, with a switch for each device, a warning when Windows blocks your microphone or camera, and a "Get this site ready for calls" button. Discord, Google Meet, Teams, Zoom, WhatsApp, Messenger and Slack get call-friendly settings by default.
- Folders open and close with an animation. New folder actions: put all tabs to sleep, collapse all folders, expand all folders.

## Improvements

- Smoother scrolling with the mouse wheel.
- Tabs can be put to sleep from their context menu, and sleeping tabs fade out.

## Fixes

- The downloads page updates on its own, without reloading it.
