## New

- Orbit beta: enter your beta key at first launch, or later in Settings › About. A key works on 3 devices and keeps working offline for 14 days.
- Orbit Mods: install reviewed mods from the Orbit store in one click. Mods made for Zen are converted to Orbit automatically.
- Orbit forum: discussions by topic, votes and a suggestions board. It opens in a tab when Orbit starts; turn it off in Settings › Browsing › Forum at launch.
- Signing in to Firefox Sync signs you in to your Orbit account on the site (once the site runs on HTTPS).

## Improvements

- Lighter, comment-free scripts.
