## New

- Picture-in-picture shows a LIVE badge and hides the seek controls on live streams.
- Closing tabs animates: the closed tab fades out and the tabs below slide up.
- Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, Max, Canal+, Crunchyroll and Apple TV show a notice explaining that Orbit can't play them. These sites need a paid DRM licence, which is too expensive for a passion project like Orbit. Use another browser to watch them.

## Improvements

- Full site isolation is back on: every site runs in its own process.
- Downloads are checked against Safe Browsing's malware list again.
- Mods can only change their own settings, not Orbit's or Firefox's.
- The nebula background uses about a fifth of the GPU it used before.
- Websites no longer see Orbit's fonts, so sites that use Inter keep their own and Orbit is harder to fingerprint.
- Extensions and mods from the Chrome Web Store and Zen's store only install over HTTPS.
- Less work in the sidebar while pages load.

## Fixes

- You no longer get signed out of sites.
- Pinned tabs stay pinned and keep their order, and clearing tabs no longer removes them.
- Tabs no longer move around on their own.
- The collapsed sidebar is square again.
- The microphone button and "people on this site" refresh correctly when you change pages.
