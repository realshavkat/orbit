## New

- Full screen (F11) now takes the whole screen. Move the pointer to the left edge to show your tabs, or to the top to show the toolbar, each on its own and floating over the page.
- Firefox Sync on the Account page: see whether you are signed in, and link your Firefox account to your Orbit account in one click. Once linked, signing in to Firefox Sync on another PC signs you in to Orbit there too.
- Sidebar text color: pick the color of tab titles, folders and the space name in Appearance, or choose your own.
- Wallpaper framing: drag your picture, GIF or video to choose what stays visible, zoom, blur, and leave space around the page (Edge, Thin, Frame, Gallery) so the wallpaper stays visible around every site.
- Most tabs kept loaded (Memory settings, 15 by default): with 50 tabs open, only the most recent stay loaded and the others sleep after 2 minutes out of sight.

## Fixes

- Your profile picture and banner now load on the Account page and in the people list.
- The people button beside the address bar is centered when nobody is on the site.
- Background changes no longer restart your wallpaper video.
