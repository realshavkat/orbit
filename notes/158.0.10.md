## Fixes

- Discord voice channels work: Orbit now reports the same browser name as Firefox, so sites that only enable features for browsers they know (Discord voice, Netflix) treat Orbit like Firefox. Sites can no longer tell Orbit apart from Firefox.
- Netflix no longer signs you out or rejects your profile PIN: Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, Max, Canal+, Crunchyroll, Apple TV, Spotify, Deezer, Twitch and YouTube are exempt from fingerprinting and strict tracking protection.
- Orbit Blocker's Stealth list no longer stands in for every blocked script, frame or request, which broke sign-in and captchas on some sites. It only stands in for known ad scripts and ad images.
- "Update lists" in Orbit Blocker's settings downloads every list again instead of only the expired ones.

## Improvements

- Pin tabs and folders exactly where you drop them: pinned tabs and pinned folders now share one list above the space name, in any order. Your pinned tabs move there automatically, in the same order.
- Collapsed folders and split views no longer throw drag and drop off.
