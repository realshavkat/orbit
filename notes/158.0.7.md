## New

- Account page in Settings: your profile at a glance, your beta key (use it on this device in one click), shortcuts to notifications, forum threads, mods and security, and who sees what.
- People on this site: a button beside the address bar shows the members here now, those who passed by recently and those who have the site as a favorite. Click someone to open their profile, or add the site to your favorites.
- Show my presence (off by default): members can see you are on a site. Only the domain is shared, never the page, and never for sensitive sites. Turning it off erases everything.

## Improvements

- Folders drag like tabs: a folder closes while you drag it, can be pinned or unpinned by dropping it above or below the space header, and reopens where you drop it.
- Appearance and Effects are now one settings page.
- The beta key screen shows the Orbit logo in white.
